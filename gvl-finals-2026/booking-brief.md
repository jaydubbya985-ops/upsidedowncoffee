## Who actually books the van

Pitch the **host club**, not the visiting club and not “the league” first.

1. **Host club canteen / facility manager** — they own the ground on finals day. Coffee vans are extra queue relief or a threat to canteen coffee. Lead with commission, not competition.
2. **GVL operations (Brad Noonan)** — confirm venue list and whether the league needs to sign off on outside vendors. Office: Kialla Park Community Centre, 18A Reserve St, Kialla. Phone **(03) 5823 9800**. AFL GM switch **(03) 5823 5021**.
3. **Council** — Tatura Rec Reserve is Greater Shepparton. If the club says yes, ask whether a casual trading / food van permit is on them or on you.

Do **not** sell pies, dimmies, or anything the canteen lives on. Coffee, hot chocolate, iced drinks, a short sweet tray. Offer **10–15% of coffee sales** or a flat site fee. Park at the **netball courts or far wing**, not in front of the canteen.
