What we bring on finals day

  • Espresso coffee, hot chocolate, iced drinks from 8am — netball and U18s eat first.
  • A short sweet tray only. No pies, no hot food, no alcohol.
  • Parked at the netball courts or far wing, not in front of the canteen.
  • Card and cash. Public liability and food-safety paperwork on request.
  • One van, one barista team, set up before the first whistle.

Club deal

10–15% of coffee sales back to the host club, or a flat site fee if that is cleaner for the treasurer. You keep all canteen and bar takings.
